How to use

Most jobs finish within a few minutes. You don’t need an account.

  1. Add your sequences

    Paste FASTA or upload a file. Keep names unique within their first 10 characters: the PHYLIP output only keeps 10.

  2. Pick the aligners

    Every selected aligner aligns your sequences independently, all at the same time. The alignments are then concatenated in random order.

  3. Download the Super-MSA

    The result page updates on its own. Download each alignment, superMSA.phylip, or everything as a zip. Bookmark the page: its link is the only way back to your results.

About wpSBOOT

Incorporating the uncertainty induced by multiple sequence alignment into bootstrap sampling can significantly increase the correlation between clade correctness and its bootstrap value. The procedure concatenates several alternative alignments of the same sequences, produced by different commonly used aligners, then draws bootstrap replicates while favouring columns of the more unique aligners. This concatenation and bootstrapping method is called Weighted Partial Super Bootstrap (wpSBOOT).

On simulated datasets of 16, 32 and 64 tips, and on 853 one-to-one orthologous genes from seven yeast species, wpSBOOT improved the discrimination between topologically correct and incorrect trees. For trees reduced at 50% and 95% bootstrap thresholds it gives the lowest Type I error (fewest false positives).

This server builds the Super-MSA, the first stage of the method.
